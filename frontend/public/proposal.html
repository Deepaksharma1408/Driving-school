🗓️ 1. Custom Booking Engine & Dispatch Workflow

A 5-step wizard built specifically for Australian driving academies to eliminate double-bookings and streamline student scheduling.

STEP 01
Package & Transmission Filter
Student selects Automatic or Manual transmission and chooses Single Lesson, Test Hire, or Combo Package.
STEP 02
Sydney Location & Suburb Filter
Filters open test routes (Botany, Silverwater, Blacktown, Marrickville) and pickup addresses.
STEP 03
Live Real-Time Slot Picker
Displays available 90-minute instructor slots in real time, preventing scheduling collisions.
STEP 04
1-Click iCal & SMS Receipts
Auto-generates booking ID, sends 24h SMS reminder, and downloads .ics calendar files for Apple/Google Calendar.
Real-Time Validation iCal (.ics) Sync Suburb Radius Filter Auto Confirmation Code

MODULE A

MODULE B
